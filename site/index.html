Things we get asked.

What does BEMO-SOLUTIONS actually do?

We help businesses modernise, automate and continuously improve how they operate. We start by understanding your business, then design and build the websites, platforms, automations, AI systems and integrations to run it better, and keep improving them through the BEMO Evolution System™.

Will we need to buy new software?

Not necessarily. We often connect and improve the tools you already have. When something new is needed, we recommend it based on your needs, budget and security, never just because we use it.

Do I need to know what technology I want?

No. You bring the business problem; we investigate, recommend and build. You don't need to understand APIs, AI models, databases or automation platforms.

Is my business too small for this?

Not at all. A small business with a serious operational problem can benefit just as much as a large organisation. We usually start with one manageable improvement and expand as your needs grow.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Most clients start with an assessment or a single, clearly scoped project. Ongoing partnerships are there if you want them, not as a requirement.

Will we be locked in to you?

No. We train your team, create SOPs and document everything we build. The goal is that your business can run its systems confidently.

How do you use AI safely?

AI is only introduced when it solves a genuine business problem. We build it with access controls, approved data sources and human review where it matters.

What's free, and what do I pay for?

The 30-minute discovery call is free, with no obligation. If you'd like to go further, such as a full Assessment or a build, we scope it with you and agree the cost upfront before any paid work starts.

What happens on the discovery call?

A relaxed 30-minute conversation about how your business runs and where it's struggling. You'll leave with a clearer picture of your options, whether or not you work with us.
